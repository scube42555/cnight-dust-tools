Generation starts when cNIGHT arrives in a new UTxO at an address with the registered stake key. Sending cNIGHT to the cold address from another wallet does that with nothing to sign. Use this self-send only for cNIGHT the cold wallet already holds. Do it after the registration is on chain.

2Build the self-send and show it to the cold machine

Every cNIGHT UTxO at the cold address is spent and everything comes back to it. Without a gift the cold wallet pays the fee (about 0.2 ADA); that needs about 1.5 ADA or more on its cNIGHT UTxOs, because the output must keep the ledger's minimum (about 1.2 ADA). With less, the gift is required and the build says so. With a gift, one small ADA-only sponsor UTxO (1 to 5 ADA) is added and its whole value goes to the cold wallet.

3Receive the payment-key witness

The cold page shows cold-payment.witness after you type SIGN there.

4Submit

A deregistration removes the stake key's registration and returns the locked ADA to the sponsor. DUST keeps being generated for cNIGHT that has not moved since, so move the cNIGHT afterwards to stop it.

To change the Dust address: (1) deregister here; (2) once that is on chain, register again in the Registration tab with a datum made on the cold page with the new Dust address; (3) once that is on chain, move the cNIGHT (the Start generation tab, or send it from another wallet). Do not register the new address first: while a stake key has two registrations, Midnight generates no DUST for it.
