DUST generation: cold signer

Starts DUST generation on Midnight for the cNIGHT held by your Cardano wallet, in two transactions. Use this page on an offline machine; it never connects to a network. A sponsor wallet on the online (hot) machine pays.

  1. Registration: signed by your stake key only. Your cNIGHT does not move.
  2. Start generation (once the registration is on chain): cNIGHT has to arrive in a new UTxO at your address. Either send cNIGHT to your address (step 2) from another wallet, with nothing to sign here, or do a self-send signed by your payment key; the page signs that only if every output comes back to your own address.
Reminder: make sure this machine is offline (cable unplugged, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth off). The browser reports a network link, but browsers say that whenever any network adapter is up, so it cannot tell whether the machine can actually reach the internet.
